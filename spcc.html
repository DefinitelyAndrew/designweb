[SPCC] Singapore Punggol Coast Corporation

4 August 2026 Games Nonfiction JD · Junior Developer
Game Description

Welcome to The SPCC Hub! This hub brings together all of our transport experiences in one place, including SPLRT Redefined, NSEWL 2024 and Sentosa Express. SPLRT Redefined is inspired by Singapore's Sengkang–Punggol LRT (SPLRT), recreating the Sengkang LRT (SKLRT) and Punggol LRT (PGLRT) networks. NSEWL 2024 is inspired by Singapore's North–South Line (NSL) and East–West Line (EWL). Sentosa Express is inspired by Singapore's iconic Sentosa Express monorail. As the hub continues to grow, even more experiences will be added, including titles from our partners.
